Our store has its roots as a metaphysical crystal shop, so rocks and crystals are a large component of our products. However, we have evolved and expanded and now offer a larger variety of products to support your health and wellness journey.

**Crystals and rocks:** We carry tumbled, raw, freeform, and carved/shaped pieces in a large variety of stone types. Our selection is always changing.....

**Jewelry:** We carry a variety of stone jewelry including earrings, pendants, and rings. We have locally created pieces by numerous local artisans.

**Herbal Products:** We carry products from Alice's Rabbit Whole that include skin salves and sprays, tinctures, syrups, teas, and greens supplement. We also carry Fuller Hives' Bee All Balm created from locally sourced beeswax and propolis.

**Clothing:** We carry a changing variety of t-shirts and sweatshirts.

**Space clearing products:** We carry white sage bundles, palo santo sticks, incense, and a variety of room sprays including white sage & palo santo.

**Oils:** We carry a variety of essential and crystal infused oil blends from Sun's Eye.

**Locally created products:** We carry framed art from Ashlie Zeidler and Robert Mayerhofer, lavender eye pillows from Ashlie Zeidler Creative, greeting cards from Under the Ash Tree and Lagom Bodywork & Inner Wellness, crystal-filled eye masks from Organics of Eastwich, orgonite pyramids from Intuitive Orgonites, copper tensor rings and other energy tools from Freeze Cat Emporium Unlimited, metalworks from Iron Garden, and simmer & spell jars from Sequoia’s Spells & Smells.

**Room decor:** Our stock of decor items is always changing based on demand and availability.
