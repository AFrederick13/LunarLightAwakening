## Flower Essence Workshop

### Sunday November 15

### 1:30 PM - 3 PM

Discover the art, energy, and vibrational qualities of flowers through the creation of your own flower essence!

Flower essences are preparations that capture the subtle energetic qualities and vibrational imprint of flowering plants. Beyond their physical properties, flowers offer another dimension of exploration through their energetic essence and relationship with water.

In this hands-on workshop, we’ll explore two approaches to flower essence creation:

* Energetic Flower Essence: Exploring the subtle energetic imprint and essence of flowers beyond conventional water-based extraction.
* Water-Based Flower Essence: Learning how flowering plants can be used to create water-infused essences that preserve their subtle vibrational qualities.

Participants will learn about the relationship between flowers, water, energy, and intention, and have the opportunity to create their own flower essence to take home.

Workshop instruction is complimentary! A materials fee of $10 will apply to cover the supplies needed for your creation.

Registration is required. Flower essence kits and materials will be prepared exclusively for registered participants.

Please contact the store to register and reserve your place!

NOTE: Ashes from a deceased loved one or pet and clothing or perfume of a deceased loved one are all acceptable to bring with for dry extraction method for dry essence jar.
