# WELCOME TO LUNAR LIGHT AWAKENING!!

It is our mission to empower individuals on their wellness journey by providing a variety of resources that support them in reaching their wellness goals. To support that mission, we are expanding the line of products and increasing accessibility to other resources that will assist customers in improving the overall health of their body, mind, and spirit. We carry a large variety of stones and crystals for aesthetic enjoyment or metaphysical use. We also stock a variety of jewelry, herbal products, books, card decks, room décor, local art, apparel, and more!

In addition to our products, we also host classes and workshops in our classroom space and partner with local practitioners to provide a variety of alternative health services in our healing spaces. We strive to provide a peaceful, welcoming environment where customers can come to shop, learn, heal, and grow!
