I am a heart-centered healer devoted to helping others reconnect with their inner truth and personal power. My path began through my work as a counselor within a Native Tribe, where I witnessed the profound connection between emotional well-being, community, and spirit. That experience awakened in me a deep respect for the wisdom found in both traditional and holistic approaches to healing.

Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of studying under a master shamanic healer and a quantum energy coach — experiences that expanded my understanding of the energetic and spiritual dimensions of healing. These teachings transformed not only my professional practice but my life as a whole, guiding me to integrate mind, body, and energy into one cohesive approach.

Today, I specialize in helping clients release the weight of past trauma, clear emotional blockages, and raise their energetic vibration so they can live with greater peace, purpose, and authenticity. My work blends trauma-informed mental health practices with intuitive energy healing, offering a uniquely holistic pathway toward transformation.

My mission is to help you uncover your innate capacity for healing — to move through fear and unresolved emotion, realign with your heart, and embody the wholeness that has always been within you.
