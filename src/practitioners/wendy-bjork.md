Wendy Bjork is a Holistic Empowerment Coach, intuitive practitioner, author, and nationally syndicated columnist who helps people reconnect with their inner wisdom and create meaningful shifts in their lives.

At the heart of Wendy’s work is The Soul Shift, a personalized transformational wellness session for those who may be feeling stuck, heavy, uncertain, or simply ready for something to change.

Each session begins with what is present for you right now. Wendy creates a grounded, supportive space to explore what may be beneath the surface, gain greater clarity, release what no longer feels aligned, and reconnect with what feels right for your next chapter.

Drawing from more than two decades of experience in holistic wellness, Wendy intuitively weaves together the tools best suited to each individual session. No previous experience with energy or soul work is needed. Simply come as you are, with a question, an area of your life you’d like to explore, or simply the feeling that something is ready to shift.
